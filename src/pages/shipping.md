## Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States. We don't ship internationally yet.

## What it costs

Shipping is free on every order.

## How long it takes

Every roll is packed to order with the capsules you picked, then shipped by ground. Once your order ships, delivery usually takes **3 to 8 business days**. Faster shipping isn't available yet.

## Tracking

We'll email you tracking details when your order ships.

## Problems with a delivery

If your order is late, lost, damaged or wrong, see [Refunds and replacements](/refunds) or email [hello@bluehournights.com](mailto:hello@bluehournights.com).
