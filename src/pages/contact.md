## Order problems

Include your order number, a short description and photos of anything missing, wrong or damaged. Problems need to be reported within 5 business days of delivery. See [Refunds and replacements](/refunds) for the details.

## If you have a reaction

Stop taking Blue Hour and talk to a doctor. If it's serious, get medical help right away, then let us know so we can look into it.

Questions about the capsules, wholesale, press or partnerships go to the same address.
