# Clarity recordings, 19–25 Nov 2026

**How this was done:** all 159 recordings from the last 7 days were played back in Clarity at 8x. For each one,
a script recorded how far down the page the visitor got, which sections they saw, and how long they stayed on each
(by playback time). Tap and FAQ events were then read for every visitor who scrolled far or tapped anything.
Canada (18 recordings, mostly the founder's own visits) is excluded below. That leaves **137 recordings, 134 from
the US.** Times are shown in Clarity's time zone (Mountain), so East Coast visitors were 2 hours later.

## The big finding

**118 of the 137 (86%) came from the two Founding Crew Instagram ads, and every one landed on /crew.**
Nobody from the ads landed on the homepage or shop. So the ads have been recruiting ambassadors, not selling rolls.
That's why this data says a lot about the Crew page and almost nothing about buyers yet.

- No recording shows a finished Crew application. One person (ezfyg7) started typing in the form and stopped.
  Check the Crew admin page to confirm.
- Only 22 of 118 (19%) scrolled far enough to see what the product is ("What your friends get").
- 4 of 118 went from /crew to the shop on their own, to find out what the product actually is.

## Groups (a visitor can be in more than one)

| Group | How many (of 118) | What they did | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 Bounced | 28 (24%) | Left in under 10 seconds, no scroll | The page didn't continue what the ad promised, or it loaded too slowly in Instagram's browser |
| G2 Read the hero, left | 61 (52%) | Stayed 10+ seconds (median 31s), never scrolled past the first screen | They read the offer and weren't convinced, or didn't understand what they'd be promoting |
| G3 Scrolled into the offer | 21 (18%) | Reached 16–60% of the page: what friends get, earnings, how it works | Interested but not sold |
| G4 Read nearly everything | 6 (5%) | Scrolled 60–100% | The highest intent. They had specific questions |
| G5 Objection checkers | 6 | Opened FAQs (see below) | Their questions are the objections everyone else had and didn't ask |
| G6 Started the form | 1 | Typed, didn't submit | The form or the commitment felt like too much at that moment |
| G7 Product-curious | 4 | Left /crew for the shop (one spent ~5 min on Supplement Facts in the builder) | Some ambassador traffic is really customer traffic. They want to know the product first |
| G8 Came back | 2 people | One came back 4 times (organic Instagram); one went ad → our profile → link in bio | The Instagram profile is part of the funnel |
| G9 Tab left open | 3 | 5+ minutes with almost no scroll | Not real reading. Ignore them in time-on-page numbers |

**The questions people tapped (G5), in order of how often:**
1. "Do I have to post?" (4 people)
2. "Can I try it first?" (3)
3. "Do I need a big following?" (3)
4. "When do I get paid?" (2)
5. "Why is 30 nights the only size?" (2)
6. "Do I have to buy anything?" (2)
7. "What if my friends think I'm selling to them?" (2)
8. "What do the first 50 get?" (1)

"Can I try it first?" and "why only 30 nights?" are the same trial and entry-price problem the ICP report found.

- One organic Instagram visitor (Oregon) came back 4 times across the homepage and /crew. That's a warm lead.
- One Miami desktop visitor went home → shop twice and tapped 9 times. Also warm.

## If they do X, we do Y (Crew page and ads)

| If they… | We… |
|---|---|
| Leave in under 10 seconds (G1) | First screen repeats the ad's exact line and shows the product. Check iOS load speed |
| Read the first screen and leave (G2) | First screen answers the top objections in one line: "No minimum following · Nothing to buy · Post when you want · Paid monthly", plus a photo of the pouch |
| Scroll into the offer (G3) | Sticky "Apply in 20 seconds" button, and answer "Can I try it first?" right there |
| Open the FAQs (G5) | Move the top 4 answers up the page. They're everyone's questions |
| Start the form and stop (G6) | Form is name + Instagram handle only; the rest comes after. Say "takes 20 seconds" |
| Go to the shop (G7) | Treat them as a possible customer: founding offer, email capture, product-first content |
| Come back through our profile (G8) | Pin 3 posts: what Blue Hour is, what's inside, the Crew deal |

## What this changes in the plan

1. **Stop paying for Crew-recruitment traffic until the Crew page is fixed.** 118 visits and no finished
   application. Recruit the first Crew members person to person (DMs, events, creators who already go out), as in
   docs/FUNNEL.md.
2. **Answer "Can I try it first?"** Approved Crew members get a few single pouches to try and hand out (split from
   one roll, about $1.50–2 a pouch). That's the founder's call on cost; see the decisions list.
3. **Lead with the product, then the money.** People went looking for what the product is before they'd promote it.
4. **The Instagram profile is part of the funnel.** At least one visitor went ad → profile → link in bio.
