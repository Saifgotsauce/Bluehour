# Blue Hour funnel plan (v2, 25 Nov 2026)

Built from the ICP research report ("ICP Deep-Dive, First 100 Offer Verdict, and Cold-Traffic Conversion
System"), the conversion report, and Clarity recordings from 19 to 25 Nov 2026 (section 9).
This replaces the old plan of boosting posts straight to a $99 checkout.

## 1. The verdict on "first 100"

- **Cold traffic: it won't convert on its own.** Strangers don't yet know what Blue Hour is, have no reason to
  trust it, and $99 from an unknown brand is a month of their live-music budget. Scarcity can't fix any of those.
  Research also shows retailer scarcity cues can *lower* credibility ("falsity inference") unless they're verifiable.
- **Warm traffic: it can close.** People who tried a pouch, follow the account, or came through a Crew member.
- **To make it work, being one of the 100 has to mean something:**
  1. A real, public counter from real paid orders ("37 of 100 founding orders claimed"). Never invented.
  2. The price really goes to $129 at order #100, and stays there.
  3. Founding member number (#001 to #100) on the order confirmation and thank-you page.
  4. Founding price locked for life on reorders.
  5. A vote on the next pouch or capsule.
  6. Opt-in "Founding 100" wall on the site.
- **The crossed-out $129 stays** (founder's decision). What keeps it honest: the cap is real, the counter is real, and
  the price genuinely goes to $129 at order #100. Never extend the cap quietly.

## 2. Who we're selling to

| S3 Crew organiser | Runs the group chat, buys tickets, books the Airbnb | Keep my people functional | Founding Crew, 3-roll price | Highest |
| S4 Soft-clubber / sober-curious | Day parties, low or no alcohol, still out late | Late night without wrecking the week | Wellness-adjacent creators, day events | Medium, cleanest for compliance |
| S5 Long shift / training | Hospitality, nurses, athletes | Short-sleep nights | Separate page, later | Test after launch |

Facts that change how we sell (sources in the report):
- 80% of US 18–29s use Instagram, 63% TikTok. Phone first: ~67% of supplement-store traffic is mobile.
- About half of Gen Z bought a supplement because it was trending. Friends and family are trusted far more than ads.
- 68% of Gen Z say going out isn't worth what it costs. They spend on the night, not on unknown add-ons.
- Only ~44% of Gen Z go out on at least half their weekends. Our core is that subset. Don't make the brand depend on
  alcohol: the soft-clubbing crowd fits "Out late. Up early." too, with far less regulatory risk.
- What turns them off: fake scarcity, countdown timers, gym-bro supplement energy, preaching, winks at drug use.
- What they reward: lo-fi and real, scene faces, brands that give back to the scene.

## 4. The system: stranger to customer

You can't buy trust at $5 a day. Borrow it from people and places they already trust, put a pouch in their hand,
and close the morning after.

**Step 0. Foundations (now, $0)**
1. 3-second clarity: hero says what it is, when you take it, why. Test on 10 strangers in the ICP: "What does this
   sell?" Keep changing it until 8 of 10 say it back. (New hero is live on the preview.)
2. Product photo: the pouch on a nightstand next to a phone charger at 4am. **Needs shooting.**
3. Trial path: OK Capsule can't make rolls smaller than 30, so the trial is a single pouch handed out
   (event welcome packs, Crew members), with a QR code that leads to a code toward a roll.
4. Trust beside the price: guarantee, free US shipping, ships from Reno, lab reports, no subscription, no stimulants.
5. Mobile checkout: Apple Pay and Google Pay are on in Stripe Checkout. Guest checkout only, no account.
6. Seed proof: 30–50 pouches with real ICP people, honest reviews and lo-fi videos, with #ad disclosure.
   First 20 reviews are worth more than any offer.

**Step 1. Awareness** (stranger to "I've seen that"), in this order:
1. Event welcome packs: a pouch at the exit or in the goodie bag. Vendor claims 15–30% buy within 30 days.
   Treat that as a best case.
2. Founding Crew members posting their own nights.
3. Micro-creators and local DJs and promoters.
4. Instagram and TikTok organic: POV, memes, "4am vs 9am".
5. $5/day boosts only on posts already doing well organically, aimed at video views, to build audiences we can
   reach again. Not to buy sales directly. (Meta limits purchase optimisation for health advertisers anyway.)
   Message: the moment first, the product second. "POV: it's 4:12am and your alarm is set for 8."

**Step 2. Interest.** A page per segment, not always the homepage: /up-early (S1), /festival (S2), /crew (S3).
**Step 3. Trust.** Lab reports, real faces, the founder story (a 22-year-old building this for his own scene is an
asset), the guarantee, "why no 5-HTP, no caffeine".
**Step 4. Trial.** Event pouch or creator code. QR on the pouch: "Scan for your morning-after code", with email
(and later SMS) opt-in.
**Step 5. First roll.** The founding offer goes here, to people who tried it or engaged:
"You tried one. 63 of 100 founding orders left at $99, price locked for life on reorders."
**Step 6. Advocacy.** Founding Crew invite on the thank-you page, with their link straight away.

Expect 5 to 8 touches before a $99 order from a new brand.

## 6. Compliance lines (every message)

- Never: hangover, cure, prevent, detox, liver, comedown, "after drinking", "safer partying", anything that suggests
  it makes alcohol or drugs safer.
- Don't headline NAC. Its legal status isn't final until the FDA's November 2026 proposed rule is finalized
  (docket FDA-2025-N-2699). It stays in The Signature, but not in hero lines, ads or captions.
- Structure/function wording with the asterisk and disclaimer. Wording in docs/CLAIMS.md.
- Crew members and creators disclose every time (#ad, "I earn commission on my code").
- Happy Tuesdays uses "come down" wording and hangover testimonials. Don't copy it. They're UK-based. We aren't
  outside the FDA's reach.

## 7. What to measure

- Comprehension: share of testers who can say what Blue Hour is after 5 seconds. Target 80%+.
- Events: pouches handed out → QR scans → opt-ins → orders, per event.
- Content: 3-second hold, 50% views, saves and shares per post.
- Site: add-to-cart rate (supplement median ~9%), checkout completion (median ~57%), orders by source.
- Founding: counter progress, days from first touch to order.

## 8. Order of work

**Phase 0, this week ($0):** clarity test with 10 strangers; publish the preview (new formula, hero, honest founding
wording, tracking) once OK Capsule confirms it can pack the 8-capsule Signature; one real test order; the
nightstand photo.
**Phase 1, seeding (~$50–120):** 1–2 rolls split into single pouches with QR cards, handed out through a US Crew
member or a pilot event; morning-after email flow; collect 15–20 honest reviews.
**Phase 2, warm close:** open the founding 100 to warm people (pouch takers, followers, Crew audiences) with the
real counter and perks.
**Phase 3, scale what worked:** boost organic winners, more events, more creators, segment pages.

## 9. What the Clarity recordings showed (19–25 Nov 2026)

Full breakdown in docs/CLARITY-2026-11.md. In short:
- 118 of 137 non-Canadian visits came from the two Founding Crew ads and all landed on /crew. The ads have been
  recruiting ambassadors, not selling rolls.
- 76% left from the first screen (24% in under 10 seconds, 52% after reading it without scrolling). Only 19% got
  far enough to see what the product is. No recording shows a finished application.
- The questions people tapped: do I have to post, can I try it first, do I need a big following, when do I get paid,
  why only 30 nights. The same trial and entry-price problem the research found.
- "Get $10 every time a friend buys" kept people about twice as far down the page as the vaguer headlines.
- iPhone visitors bounced far more than Android (23 of 71 vs 4 of 46). Check load speed in Instagram's browser.

## 10. Decisions only Saif can make

1. **Pilot events:** charge $99 a roll as now, or give the first 1–2 events their pouches free to get data and
   reviews faster? (The report recommends free pilots in exchange for exit handout, a Story mention and a code.)
2. **Seeding budget:** about $50–120 for 1–2 rolls split into single pouches for Crew members and pilots.
   Needs a US person to receive them (OK Capsule doesn't ship to Canada).
3. **Guarantee:** keep "no refunds unless something's wrong", or add a first-roll guarantee? Each refunded roll
   costs about $40–60 plus Stripe's fee. The report counts this as a main trust fix.
4. **Founding perks:** founding price locked for life on reorders? A vote on the next capsule? (Numbered founding
   members are already built.)
5. **Order now vs pre-order:** switch once OK Capsule confirms it can pack the new 8-capsule Signature right away.
6. **Crew ads:** pause paid Crew ads until the Crew page is fixed, and recruit person to person first?
